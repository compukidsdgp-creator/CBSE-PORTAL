Learning objective

To investigate Cone & Slant Height Explorer using a dynamic mathematical model and justify a conclusion from recorded values.

Mathematical principle

l = √(r²+h²) · CSA = πrl · V = ⅓πr²h

Procedure

1
Enter student details and note the initial settings.
2
Change one independent variable while keeping the others fixed.
3
Observe the visual model and note the calculated values.
4
Repeat with at least three different settings and record the readings.
5
Use the pattern in your observations to write a mathematical conclusion.

Variables

Independent: the control being changed. Dependent: calculated result. Controlled: other settings held constant.

Guided investigation: Change r and h and compare slant height, curved area and volume.

Common errors to avoid

  • Changing several variables simultaneously.
  • Recording rounded values before completing calculations.
  • Confusing linear, square and cubic units.
  • Writing a conclusion that is not supported by observations.
