Mathematical idea

A linear function has the form y = mx + c. The coefficient m is the slope (rate of change), and c is the y-intercept. For every unit increase in x, y changes by m units.

m = (y₂ − y₁) / (x₂ − x₁)   and   y = mx + c

Aim and procedure

1
Set an initial slope and intercept. Predict how the line will appear.
2
Change only the slope. Record the direction and steepness of the line.
3
Restore the slope and change the intercept. Observe the vertical shift.
4
Select at least three x-values and calculate corresponding y-values.
5
Compare your observations with the equation and write a conclusion.
