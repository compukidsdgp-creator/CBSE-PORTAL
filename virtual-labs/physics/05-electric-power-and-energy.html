6 · Guided experimental procedure

Follow the sequence below and use the original experiment controls above to change one variable at a time.

  1. Read the objective and identify the independent and dependent variables.
  2. Inspect the apparatus and reset the simulation to its initial state.
  3. Change only the independent variable while keeping other conditions controlled.
  4. Record at least three observations in the notebook.
  5. Plot the dependent variable against the independent variable and describe the trend.
  6. Answer the prediction and fault-finding questions, then write an evidence-based conclusion.
Experimental discipline
Use consistent units, avoid changing multiple variables at once, and distinguish measured observations from theoretical predictions.
