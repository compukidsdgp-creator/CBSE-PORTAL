SCIENTIFIC INSIGHT

CONCEPT CHECK
Why does pH matter?

pH expresses how acidic or basic an aqueous solution is. It is related to hydrogen ion concentration.

Key relationship

pH = −log₁₀[H⁺] (for dilute aqueous solutions under the usual school-level approximation).
