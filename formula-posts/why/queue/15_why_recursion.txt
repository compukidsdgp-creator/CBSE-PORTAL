🔁 WHY? — How does RECURSION know when to stop?

def fact(n):
    if n == 1: return 1        # base case 🛑
    return n * fact(n - 1)

fact(4) → fact(3) → fact(2) → fact(1) 🛑
then back up: 2 × 1 = 2 → 3 × 2 = 6 → 4 × 6 = 24 ✅

No base case = it never stops → RecursionError ❌
