🍦 WHY? — Why is a cone's volume ⅓ of a cylinder's?

Same base, same height 📏
Fill the cone with water → pour into the cylinder → ⅓ full 💧
Again → ⅔ … once more → FULL! 3 cones = 1 cylinder ✨
So V(cone) = ⅓ πr²h
