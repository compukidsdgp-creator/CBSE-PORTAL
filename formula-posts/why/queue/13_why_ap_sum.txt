➕ WHY? — Why is the AP sum Sₙ = n/2 (a + l)?

Draw 1, 2, 3 … 6 as columns of blocks → a staircase 🪜
Copy it, flip it, fit it on top → a perfect 6 × 7 rectangle! ▭
Rectangle = 42 blocks → the staircase is HALF = 21 ✨
So Sₙ = n/2 × (first + last)
