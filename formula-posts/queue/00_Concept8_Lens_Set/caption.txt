🔍 Concept in 8 Seconds — Physics | Lens Formula

Where will the image form? 🕯️➡️🔍➡️❓
A candle is 30 cm in front of a convex lens of focal length 10 cm. Swipe ➡️ to solve it step by step.

1️⃣ The question
2️⃣ The lens formula: 1/v − 1/u = 1/f
3️⃣ Put in the values (f = +10 cm, u = −30 cm)
4️⃣ The answer: v = +15 cm (real image, on the other side)
5️⃣ Quick recap

⚠️ Board-exam trap: 1/f = 1/v + 1/u is the MIRROR formula. For a lens it is 1/v − 1/u = 1/f.
