Learn: the WHY? behind CBSE concepts

Board questions reward students who understand why a formula or idea works, not just what it says. Each explainer here takes one concept from the Class 10 or Class 12 syllabus, explains it step by step with a worked example and common mistakes, and ends with practice questions. Every article comes with a short WHY? reel. Written by Biswarup Mukherjee (Teaching Administrator, CBSEMock) and reviewed by our subject teachers. New explainers are added every Sunday and Wednesday.

Computer Science CBSE Class 12Why is 0.1 + 0.2 not equal to 0.3 in Python?Why Python prints 0.30000000000000004 for 0.1 + 0.2 — binary fractions, floating-point rounding, and the right way to compare decimals (round, math.isclose). CBSE Class 12 CS.
AI CBSE Class 10 · 12How does an AI neuron make a decision?Inputs, weights, weighted sum and threshold — how a single artificial neuron decides, with a worked example. Neural networks explained for CBSE AI Class 10 and 12.
Maths CBSE Class 10Why is sin²θ + cos²θ = 1?Prove sin²θ + cos²θ = 1 with one unit-circle picture and Pythagoras — plus the two related identities, worked examples and practice for CBSE Class 10.
Computer Science CBSE Class 12Why is binary search so fast?How binary search finds one item among 1,000 in about 10 steps — halving, log₂ n, a Python implementation and exam tips for CBSE Class 12 Computer Science.
AI CBSE Class 10 · 12How does a computer see an image?Pixels, RGB numbers, filters and edges — how computer vision lets a machine recognise a face. A clear explainer for the CBSE AI curriculum (Class 10 and 12).
Maths CBSE Class 10Why is the area of a circle πr²?See why the area of a circle is πr² by unrolling it into a triangle — a picture proof for CBSE Class 10 Maths, with a worked example and practice questions.
