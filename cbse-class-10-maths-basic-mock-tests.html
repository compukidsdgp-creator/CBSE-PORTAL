Q1. The HCF of 8 and 12 is:
  1. 2
  2. 4
  3. 8
  4. 24

Answer: (B) 4 — 8 = 2³, 12 = 2² × 3; HCF = 2² = 4

Q2. The LCM of 6 and 9 is:
  1. 3
  2. 9
  3. 18
  4. 54

Answer: (C) 18 — 6 = 2×3, 9 = 3²; LCM = 2×3² = 18

Q3. Which of the following is a quadratic polynomial?
  1. 2x + 3
  2. x² - 5
  3. x³ + x
  4. 5

Answer: (B) x² - 5 — degree 2, exactly one variable squared; no higher powers.

Q4. The zeroes of the polynomial p(x) = x² - 9 are:
  1. ±3
  2. ±9
  3. 3, 9
  4. -3, 9

Answer: (A) ±3 — x² - 9 = 0 → x² = 9 → x = ±3

Q5. If 2x + 3y = 12 and x - y = 1, then x = ?
  1. 3
  2. 4
  3. 2
  4. 5

Answer: (A) 3 — From x - y = 1: x = y + 1. Substitute: 2(y+1) + 3y = 12 → 5y = 10 → y = 2 → x = 3

Q6. The value of k for which the equation kx² + 6x + 1 = 0 has real and equal roots is:
  1. 9
  2. 6
  3. 3
  4. 12

Answer: (A) 9 — D = 0: b² - 4ac = 0 → 36 - 4k = 0 → k = 9

Q7. The 5th term of an AP with first term 2 and common difference 3 is:
  1. 11
  2. 14
  3. 17
  4. 8

Answer: (B) 14 — a₅ = 2 + (5-1)×3 = 2 + 12 = 14

Q8. The sum of first 10 natural numbers is:
  1. 50
  2. 55
  3. 60
  4. 45

Answer: (B) 55 — S = n(n+1)/2 = 10×11/2 = 55

Q9. Find the HCF of 96 and 404 using the Euclidean algorithm.

Model answer: 404 = 4 × 96 + 20 96 = 4 × 20 + 16 20 = 1 × 16 + 4 16 = 4 × 4 + 0 HCF(96, 404) = 4

Q10. Prove that 3 + 2√5 is irrational.

Model answer: Assume 3 + 2√5 is rational. Then 3 + 2√5 = p/q (p, q integers, q ≠ 0, coprime) 2√5 = p/q - 3 = (p - 3q)/q √5 = (p - 3q)/(2q) Since p, q are integers, (p-3q)/(2q) is rational. But √5 is irrational (proven/given). Contradiction! ∴ 3 + 2√5 is irrational. ✓

Q11. If the sum of the first n terms of an AP is given by Sₙ = 5n - n², find the first term and common difference. Also find the 10th term.

Model answer: Sₙ = 5n - n² a₁ = S₁ = 5(1) - 1² = 4 S₂ = 5(2) - 4 = 6 → a₂ = S₂ - S₁ = 6 - 4 = 2 d = a₂ - a₁ = 2 - 4 = -2 a₁₀ = a + 9d = 4 + 9(-2) = 4 - 18 = -14

Q12. Case Study 1: A student scores in Math tests: 65, 70, 72, 68, 75, 80, 72, 65, 70, 78. (i) Find the mean score. (ii) Find the mode. (iii) Arrange in order and find the median. (iv) Which measure best represents typical performance?

Model answer: (i) Mean = (65+70+72+68+75+80+72+65+70+78)/10 = 715/10 = 71.5 [1 mark] (ii) Mode: 65 appears 2×, 70 appears 2×, 72 appears 2× → three modes: 65, 70, 72 [bimodal analysis — or 'no single mode'] [1 mark] (iii) Ordered: 65, 65, 68, 70, 70, 72, 72, 75, 78, 80 Median = (5th + 6th)/2 = (70 + 72)/2 = 71 [1 mark] (iv) Mean (71.5) or Median (71) best represents typical performance; they are close. Mode is less useful when multiple modes exist. [1 mark]
