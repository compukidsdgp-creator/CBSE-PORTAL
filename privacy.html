Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how CBSEMock.org.in ("we", "our", or "the Site") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit our website. We are committed to protecting your privacy and complying with applicable data protection laws.

1. Information We Collect

We do not require users to register or create accounts. We collect limited data automatically:

  • Usage data: Pages visited, time spent, browser type, device type, and approximate location (country/city level) — collected via Google Analytics 4.
  • Cookies: Small text files placed by Google Analytics and Google AdSense to understand usage patterns and serve relevant advertisements.
  • Log data: Standard server logs including IP address, browser, and referring page — retained for security purposes only.

We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any personally identifiable information unless you voluntarily contact us.

2. Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand how visitors use our site. Google Analytics collects anonymised usage data through cookies. You can opt out by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

3. Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google AdSense uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info.

AdSense Publisher ID: ca-pub-2810600896966939

4. Cookies

Our site uses the following types of cookies:

  • Analytics cookies: Google Analytics (_ga, _gid, _gat) — track page views and user behaviour anonymously.
  • Advertising cookies: Google AdSense — serve and measure advertisements.
  • Preference cookies: Theme preference (light/dark mode) stored locally in your browser.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect site functionality.

5. Children's Privacy

Our site is designed for students preparing for CBSE board examinations (Class 9–12). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it immediately.

Google AdSense is configured for non-personalised ads in compliance with policies regarding users under 18.

6. Third-Party Links

Our site may contain links to external websites (CBSE official site, news sources, etc.). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites and encourage you to review their privacy policies.

7. Social Media

We maintain a Facebook page and Telegram channel. If you interact with these platforms, their respective privacy policies apply:

  • Facebook / Meta Privacy Policy
  • Telegram Privacy Policy

8. User Data Deletion

Since we do not collect personal accounts or user profiles, there is no account data to delete. For data collected via Google Analytics or AdSense cookies, you can:

  • Clear cookies from your browser settings at any time.
  • Use the Google Analytics opt-out tool.
  • Manage Google ad personalisation at myaccount.google.com.

To request deletion of any data you believe we hold about you, email us at: admin@cbsemock.org.in. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical measures to protect the site and any data we process. However, no internet transmission is completely secure. We encourage users not to share sensitive personal information through any website contact form.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact Us

For privacy-related questions or data deletion requests:

  • Email: admin@cbsemock.org.in
  • Website: cbsemock.org.in
