About CBSEMock

Free, exam-focused practice for CBSE Class 10 and Class 12 students — built by teachers in Durgapur, West Bengal.

What we do

CBSEMock.org.in helps students prepare for the CBSE board exams with material that looks and feels like the real paper. Everything on the site is free to use, with no sign-up.

  • Full-length mock papers for the major Class 10 and Class 12 subjects, laid out like the latest CBSE sample papers, with answers after you submit.
  • Fresh pre-board papers every Monday, Wednesday and Friday — one for Class 10 and one for Class 12 — written so that no question repeats before the board exams.
  • Chapter-wise quizzes with instant feedback and an explanation for every answer.
  • Study notes, interactive virtual labs for Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Mathematics, Computer Science and AI, formula cards and short explainer videos.

Who we are

CBSEMock is run by a small, independent team of teachers in Durgapur, West Bengal, who teach Science, Mathematics, Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence to school students every day. We built the site because our own students kept asking the same thing before exams: “Where can I get more papers that are exactly like the board exam?”

How our content is made

  • Syllabus first. Every paper and quiz follows the rationalised CBSE syllabus and the official sample-paper pattern for the 2026–27 session. Topics removed from the syllabus are left out.
  • Teacher-approved. Question papers are reviewed and approved by the respective subject teachers before they are published.
  • No repeats. New mock-paper questions are checked automatically against all of our earlier questions and against published board questions before a paper is scheduled.
  • Worked answers. Questions come with an answer key and, wherever useful, a short explanation of the method, so students learn from mistakes rather than just seeing a score.
  • Corrections. If you spot an error, tell us. We fix reported mistakes as quickly as we can, usually within two days, and update the page date.

How the site is funded

The site is free for students. To cover hosting and the time it takes to keep producing new papers, some pages may show advertising. Ads never affect what we publish, and we never ask students for payment or personal details to use the material. See our Privacy Policy for how cookies and analytics are used.
