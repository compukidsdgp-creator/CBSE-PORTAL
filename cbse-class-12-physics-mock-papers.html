Q1. A hollow conducting sphere of radius R has charge Q. If the radius is doubled keeping charge same, the electric flux through the Gaussian surface enclosing the sphere will:
  1. increase
  2. decrease
  3. remain the same
  4. become zero

Answer: (C) remain the same

Q2. Two resistors of 1000 W each are connected in parallel across 220 V. Total power consumed is:
  1. 500 W
  2. 1000 W
  3. 2000 W
  4. 4000 W

Answer: (C) 2000 W

Q3. A metallic cylinder is placed in a uniform magnetic field with its axis parallel to the field. If the field changes with time, induced current in the cylinder is:
  1. along its axis
  2. circular inside
  3. no current will be induced
  4. along surface only

Answer: (C) no current will be induced

Q4. An electromagnetic wave propagating along x-axis has equation E = E₀cos(kx−ωt). Direction of propagation is:
  1. +x̂
  2. −x̂
  3. ŷ
  4. ẑ

Answer: (A) +x̂

Q5. Which of the following cannot be explained by Huygens' wave theory of light?
  1. Reflection
  2. Refraction
  3. Interference
  4. Origin of spectra

Answer: (D) Origin of spectra

Q6. A distant star sends light to Earth. The wavefront reaching Earth is:
  1. Spherical
  2. Cylindrical
  3. Plane
  4. Elliptical

Answer: (C) Plane

Q7. The binding energy per nucleon is maximum for nuclei near iron (Fe-56). This means these nuclei:
  1. undergo spontaneous fission
  2. are most tightly bound
  3. are least stable
  4. cannot participate in nuclear reactions

Answer: (B) are most tightly bound

Q8. A superconducting ball cooled below critical temperature in a magnetic field. On removing field, ball will:
  1. attract the bar magnet
  2. repel the bar magnet
  3. show no interaction
  4. align with field

Answer: (D) align with field

Q9. An EM wave has frequency ν = 10¹⁸ Hz and travels in vacuum. Find: (i) refractive index of medium if speed is 2×10⁸ m/s, (ii) angular frequency ω.

Model answer: (i) μ = c/v = 3×10⁸/2×10⁸ = 1.5 (ii) ω = 2πν = 2π×10¹⁸ rad/s; frequency corresponds to X-ray range.

Q10. (i) Compare electrical conductivity of metals, insulators and semiconductors based on band theory. (ii) Explain why diamond is insulator but tin is conductor though both are Group 14.

Model answer: Metals: Overlapping/partially filled conduction band → free electrons → high conductivity. Insulators: Large band gap (>5 eV) → negligible thermal excitation → poor conductivity. Semiconductors: Moderate band gap (~1 eV) → some carriers at room temp → intermediate conductivity. Diamond: Very large band gap (~5.4 eV) → insulator. Tin: Metallic bonding, overlapping bands → conductor.

Q11. [CBQ] Polymer Semiconductor Wearables A startup designs flexible wearable patches using polymer semiconductors. The device must operate with minimal power and withstand bending. (i) What property makes these devices low power? [1m] (ii) Why is diamond NOT used as semiconductor in such devices? [1m] (iii) In intrinsic polymer semiconductor, compare number of electrons and holes. [1m] (iv) Explain role of majority and minority carriers in polymer semiconductor conductivity. [4m — extended]

Model answer: (i) Low power consumption and reliability: polymer semiconductors have moderate band gap → low energy needed. (ii) Diamond's band gap (~5.4 eV) is too large for thermal excitation at room temperature → not a semiconductor. (iii) In intrinsic semiconductor: electrons = holes (generated in pairs). (iv) Majority carriers (electrons in n-type, holes in p-type) dominate conduction. Minority carriers play role in recombination. Balance allows low-power operation even when bent/stretched.

Q12. (A) A parallel plate capacitor has plates of area A separated by d, with dielectric K between them. (i) An isolated conductor always has small capacitance — why? [2m] (ii) Three capacitors 6μF, 9μF, 18μF: find minimum and maximum capacitance. [2m] (iii) Two other factors (besides area) on which capacitance depends. [1m]

Model answer: (i) C = 4πε₀R for isolated sphere → depends only on radius. Very large capacitance needs impractically large sphere. (ii) Min (series): 1/C = 1/6+1/9+1/18 = 6/18 → C_min = 3 μF. Max (parallel): C_max = 6+9+18 = 33 μF. (iii) Distance between plates (C ∝ 1/d), Permittivity of medium (C ∝ ε/K).
